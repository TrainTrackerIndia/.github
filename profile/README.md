# 🚆 TrainTracker

TrainTracker is a modern, ad-free railway tracking platform built for **India**.

Inspired by the live exploration experience of flight-tracking platforms and the practical journey tools of Indian railway apps, TrainTracker brings railway tracking, journey information, and exploration together in one clean experience.

> **Currently in development.**

---

## 🗺️ What We're Building

### 🌍 Live Railway Map

Explore the Indian railway network through an interactive map.

- Railway lines
- Stations
- Live train markers
- Train direction
- Train activity
- Search directly from the map
- Tap trains and stations for details
- Follow trains
- Nearby trains
- Map layers
- Light and dark map modes

---

### 🚆 Train Tracking

Track individual trains and view their running information.

- Train number
- Train name
- Train type
- Origin & destination
- Current location
- Last reported station
- Next station
- Delay
- Estimated arrival
- Journey progress
- Full route
- Scheduled vs estimated timings

---

### 📍 Where Is My Train

A dedicated journey tracking experience.

See:

- Current train position
- Passed stations
- Current station
- Upcoming stations
- Next station
- Delay
- ETA
- Journey progress
- Complete route

---

### 🚃 Coach & Seat Guide

Know where your coach and seat are before the train arrives.

- Coach composition
- Coach order
- Engine direction
- Coach type
- Seat/berth number
- Berth type
- Coach layout
- Highlighted seat
- Platform information where available
- Approximate coach stopping position where reliable data exists

> Platform and coach-position information will be clearly identified when it is estimated rather than confirmed.

---

### 🚉 Station Information

Every supported station can have its own information page.

- Station name
- Station code
- Arrivals
- Departures
- Upcoming trains
- Delays
- Platforms where available
- Routes through the station
- Nearby trains
- Live station activity
- Favorite stations

#### 📡 Live Station Board

View arrivals and departures in a clean railway-board-style interface.

---

### 🧭 Journey Planner

Find trains between stations.

Enter:

**From → To → Date**

Then view:

- Available trains
- Departure time
- Arrival time
- Journey duration
- Number of stops
- Service status
- Delay information
- Connections where supported

---

### 🎫 PNR Status

Connect your booking with your journey.

- PNR lookup
- Booking status
- Journey date
- Train information
- Passenger booking status
- Coach & berth information where available
- Confirmed / RAC / waitlist status
- Link directly to live train tracking
- Connect PNR → Journey → Coach & Seat Guide

PNR functionality will only use legitimate and reliable data sources.

---

### ⭐ Favorites & Following

Save the things you care about.

- Favorite trains
- Favorite stations
- Favorite routes
- Saved journeys
- Follow trains
- Followed-train map view

---

### 🔔 Alerts

Optional notifications for important journey events.

- Train departing soon
- Train approaching your station
- Delay detected
- Schedule changes
- Cancellation
- Platform changes where reliable data is available
- Journey completed

---

### 📊 Train Statistics

Explore historical train performance when sufficient reliable data is available.

- Average delay
- Typical journey duration
- Historical arrival performance
- Station-specific delay patterns
- Service history

---

### ⏪ Historical Replay

Replay previous train journeys when historical position data is available.

Select a train and date and watch its recorded journey unfold on the map.
